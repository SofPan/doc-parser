Target User: Insurance Broker

Insurance brokers regularly handle high volumes of diverse policy documents, endorsement forms, and binders from multiple carriers. To onboard clients or review coverage, brokers must review these documents and capture critical data points such as policy numbers, coverage limits, deductibles, effective dates, and exclusions.

Currently, this data extraction is an entirely manual process. Because different insurance carriers use unstructured layouts, varying terminologies, and unpredictable document formats, traditional rule-based software cannot reliably parse the text. Brokers are forced to meticulously read through multi-page PDFs to find and re-key information into their management systems.

This manual entry creates significant operational bottlenecks. Manual data entry consumes significant amounts of brokers' time, particularly when reviewing lengthy or inconsistently formatted documents. Human Error can result in critical coverage mismatches, processing delays, or compliance liabilities. Slow ingestion times delay client onboarding and quote comparisons, risking lost business to faster competitors.

The objective is to eliminate manual data entry by introducing an intelligent ingestion system. The solution must automatically accept unstructured policy documents, accurately identify and extract key insurance entities regardless of formatting variations, and format the data for seamless system entry. This will reduce processing time per document while maintaining data accuracy.
